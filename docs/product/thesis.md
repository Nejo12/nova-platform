# Product Thesis

Tessnova provides one shared technology platform underneath profession-specific storefront and client-acquisition experiences.

```text
Platform Infrastructure
        ↓
Business Capabilities
        ↓
Vertical Schemas + Rules + Themes
        ↓
Profession-specific Product Experience
```

## Platform infrastructure
Accounts, authentication, workspaces, publishing, domains, media, email transport, analytics ingestion, payment rails, subscription billing, feature flags and AI infrastructure.

## Reusable business capabilities
Portfolio, services, pricing, testimonials, enquiries, leads, availability, bookings, deposits, downloads, locations, newsletter and lightweight CRM.

## Vertical layer
Each vertical supplies profession-specific terminology, required fields, rules, onboarding, analytics interpretation, SEO defaults, themes and workflows.
