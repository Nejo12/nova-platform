# Tessnova Brand Brief

## Status

**Founder-approved product brand:** Tessnova  
**Engineering repository:** `nova-platform`

The name originated from the founding validation verticals:
- **T** — Tattoo
- **E** — Events
- **S** — Speakers

The acronym is an origin story, not a permanent product taxonomy.

## Thesis

Independent professionals do not need another generic page builder. They need a professional presence designed around how customers in their profession evaluate, trust, enquire and book them.

## Promise

**Make your work easy to see, trust and choose.**

Commercial interpretation:

**Turn your work into enquiries, bookings and clients.**

For Tattoo V1, the sharper working proposition is:

**Turn social attention into qualified tattoo enquiries and safe flash reservations.**

## Personality

Creative, confident, calm, professional, opinionated, precise and human.

Avoid:
- generic AI aesthetics;
- creator-hustle language;
- corporate enterprise tone;
- unnecessary technical language;
- template sameness.

## Brand architecture

Tessnova belongs to the Nova product family alongside Nova UI, Klinnova and Slotnova.

Tessnova is a **separate product**. Do not put Tattoo functionality into Klinnova or Slotnova.

## Domain

`tessnova.de` is owned and active.

## Name decision / legal status

The Founder reviewed the similar commercial name **Tesnova Solutions** and explicitly approved proceeding with Tessnova.

This similarity is **not a product blocker** and must not silently reopen the naming debate without new material evidence.

If/when a formal trademark filing or material long-term brand investment is undertaken, perform appropriate German/EU trademark due diligence and professional legal review where useful.
