# Tessnova Brand Brief v0.2

## Status
**Approved M0 brand:** Tessnova  
**Engineering codename:** `nova-platform`

The name originated from the founding verticals:
- **T** — Tattoo
- **E** — Events
- **S** — Speakers

The acronym is an origin story, not a permanent product taxonomy.

## Thesis
Independent professionals do not need another generic page builder. They need a professional presence designed around how customers in their profession evaluate, trust, enquire and book them.

## Promise
**Make your work easy to see, trust and choose.**

Commercial interpretation: **Turn your work into enquiries, bookings and clients.**

## Personality
Creative, confident, calm, professional, opinionated, precise and human.

Avoid generic AI aesthetics, creator-hustle language, corporate enterprise tone and unnecessarily technical language.

## Brand architecture
Tessnova belongs to the Nova product family alongside Nova UI, Klinnova and Slotnova.

## Clearance status
Tessnova is approved for planning, design and validation. Commercial launch and material brand spend remain gated on formal German/EU company, domain and trademark review.
