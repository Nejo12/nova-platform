# M1 Customer Interview Guide

## Objective
Understand current acquisition, qualification and booking workflows without pitching Tessnova prematurely.

## Core questions
1. Tell me about your business and the type of clients you want more of.
2. Show me how your last five enquiries arrived.
3. What information did each client give you initially?
4. What did you have to ask for afterward?
5. Which tools did you use from first contact to confirmed booking?
6. Where do enquiries get lost, delayed or become frustrating?
7. What questions do you answer repeatedly?
8. What information must a customer see before contacting you?
9. What makes you reject or ignore an enquiry?
10. What software do you currently pay for?
11. If one part of this process disappeared tomorrow, which would save you the most time?
12. How do you currently know whether your Instagram/site/profile produces actual business?

## Do not lead with
- "Would you use Tessnova?"
- feature lists
- pricing before understanding existing spend and pain

## Evidence to capture
Acquisition source, current tools, repeated manual steps, missing lead information, booking value, monthly enquiry volume, current spend and strongest pain.
