# M1 Recruitment Pack

## Purpose

Recruit professionals for workflow interviews without pitching Tessnova or priming them toward a particular solution.

## General outreach

Hi — I’m researching how independent professionals manage their online presence, enquiries and bookings.

I’m working on a new product concept, but at this stage I’m not selling anything. I’m trying to understand how people actually work today, which tools they use, and where the process becomes frustrating.

Would you be open to a short 20–30 minute conversation? I’d mainly ask you to walk me through how your recent enquiries and bookings happened in practice.

## Tattoo outreach

Hi — I’m researching how independent tattoo artists currently manage Instagram/website enquiries, books-open periods, flash, deposits and client information.

I’m not trying to sell anything. I’m looking for tattoo artists willing to walk me through how their real booking process works today.

Would you have 20–30 minutes for a short conversation?

## Wedding / event outreach

Hi — I’m researching how independent wedding/event professionals turn Instagram, Google or marketplace interest into qualified enquiries and confirmed bookings.

I’m not selling anything. I’d like to understand the real workflow: what clients ask, what information you need, which tools you use, and where enquiries get difficult.

Would you be open to a 20–30 minute conversation?

## Speaker outreach

Hi — I’m researching how professional speakers and experts are discovered, evaluated and booked by event organizers.

I’m not selling anything. I’m interested in the real process: enquiries, topics, speaker reels, media kits, fees, availability and the back-and-forth before a booking is confirmed.

Would you be open to a 20–30 minute conversation?

## Screening fields

Capture:
- profession / role;
- city / primary market;
- solo vs team;
- main acquisition channels;
- current website/profile;
- booking/enquiry software currently used;
- approximate monthly enquiry volume;
- willingness to show their real workflow/screens.

## Research rule

Do not describe Tessnova features before the workflow portion of the interview is complete.

The goal is to discover the existing problem, not obtain approval for our proposed solution.
