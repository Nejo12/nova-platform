# EVT-02 — Wedding / event florist, Hamburg

**Status:** Interviewed  
**Evidence quality:** Strong  
**Source:** Founder-collected response

## Profile

Studio florist in Hamburg, small team of 2, ~25 weddings/year, mostly weddings of 60–100 guests, plus some corporate events.

## Discovery

- Instagram 50%
- venue recommendations 30%
- Google 10%
- other vendors remainder

## Current workflow

Long email + Pinterest board + venue
→ manual calendar check
→ starting-price PDF
→ 30-minute call
→ proposal
→ contract
→ 30% bank-transfer deposit

## Information clients want

- previous work
- rough price level
- venue familiarity
- date availability
- delivery

## Qualification fields

- date
- venue
- guest count
- budget range
- style

Budget is the strongest qualifier.

## Current tools

Instagram, Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion proposal board, bank transfer, occasional WhatsApp.

## Strong pain

### Budget mismatch discovered too late
A €500 budget can emerge only after a call for a €3,000 concept.

### Funnel drop-off
~40% disappear after the PDF; participant assumes price is the main reason.

### Existing site is brochure-only
Squarespace looks good but contact form is only name + message.

### Informal date holds
Dates are held manually for about a week.

## Product reaction

Desired:
- portfolio by style
- “from €X”
- date availability / date field
- budget-range qualification

Would reject:
- template-looking product;
- monthly fee that scales with enquiry volume.

## Pricing evidence

> €50/month is too much unless contracts and payments are included.

## Jobs-to-be-done

1. Filter unviable budgets before a call.
2. Capture date, venue, budget and guest count immediately.
3. Present premium work without looking templated.
4. Reduce brochure/PDF dependency.
