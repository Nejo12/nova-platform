# EVT-01 — Wedding florist, Berlin / Brandenburg

**Status:** Interviewed  
**Evidence quality:** Strong  
**Source:** Founder-collected response

## Business context

Wedding florist serving Berlin / Brandenburg. Typical weddings are 40–90 guests and booked 8–14 months in advance.

## Discovery

Approximate order:
1. Instagram
2. planners
3. venue referrals
4. Google usually validates the name after discovery rather than starting the journey

## What couples need before enquiry

- recent weddings
- travel/service area
- a starting-price signal

Exact price lists remain private.

Useful public price-floor examples:
- bridal bouquets from about €170
- full floral design from about €150 per guest

The full-install minimum is approximately €2,200.

## Qualification fields

- date
- venue
- guest count
- ceremony + dinner vs dinner only
- budget band
- photos helpful, but moodboard not required initially

## Current toolchain

Instagram
→ email
→ Google Calendar
→ Canva PDF guide
→ Lexoffice invoice

Calls are arranged manually by email.

## Strong pain

### Late / incomplete qualification
The PDF is sent only after date, venue and guest count are known.

### Budget mismatch
Publishing no floor produces enquiries far below the minimum.

### Slow follow-up loses leads
Common drop-off is after the PDF and before a call, or when the enquiry arrives during a wedding weekend and the quote goes out days later.

### Scope drift
Guest count can materially increase after initial enquiry, forcing a quote redo.

## Public availability

No public calendar desired.

June Saturdays collide operationally because setup/travel can make a nominally free date unavailable.

## Product reaction

Desired public experience:
- recent work
- travel area
- starting prices
- date field
- guest count
- venue
- budget band

One complete wedding case with actual cost can be more persuasive than generic reviews.

Strong visual requirement:
A dull, generic or obvious AI-look product would be rejected immediately.

## Pricing evidence

- monthly subscription preferred over one-time fee
- €29/month: comparison point against domain + PDF tooling
- €49/month: too much unless deposit + contract draft are included

## Jobs-to-be-done

1. Qualify wedding enquiries before time is spent on proposals.
2. Filter out clients far below the minimum budget.
3. Present recent, premium work credibly.
4. Reduce response delay and back-and-forth before a call/proposal.

## Candidate product implications

- Date/venue/guest-count/budget qualification is the core flow.
- Availability is operational, not a public slot picker.
- Visual quality is itself a purchase criterion.
- Deposits/contracts are expansion value, not necessarily V1.
