# SPK-01 — Independent speaker, product & engineering leadership

**Status:** Interviewed  
**Evidence quality:** Strong  
**Source:** Founder-collected response

## Business context

Independent speaker focused on product and engineering leadership.

Typical talks:
- 30–45 minutes
- company offsites
- one or two conferences per year

Typical company-offsite fee: €4,500 + travel.

## Current workflow

LinkedIn discovery
→ organizer asks for bio + video
→ reel + short bio + topic blurbs sent
→ organizer asks for long bio + landscape photo
→ date + city arrive later
→ fee accepted
→ 50% invoice

Mostly direct booking.

## What organizers need

- topics
- reel
- audience fit
- fee
- travel terms
- date
- city
- audience size
- budget often arrives late or not at all

## Repeated asset problem

Same assets sent repeatedly:
- 80-word bio
- 250-word bio
- three photos
- two talk descriptions
- PDF one-sheet

The PDF becomes stale and can list talks no longer offered.

## Strong pain

### Fragmented professional assets
Reel, bios, photos and topics live in different places.

### Event constraints arrive late
Availability is not a simple calendar yes/no.

Example request:
“Are you free 12–13 November in Munich, arrival the night before?”

## Product reaction

Would replace LinkedIn link if one page had:
- current talks
- reel
- travel terms
- event enquiry form asking date/city/audience/budget

Would reject:
- public fee table
- inability to use a personal domain

Shared templates are acceptable.

## Pricing evidence

- €19/month: easy yes
- €39/month: still acceptable
- above €60/month: too much because the speaking site is not the primary revenue system

## Jobs-to-be-done

1. Keep professional speaking assets current in one place.
2. Reduce repeated emailing of bios, photos, topics and reel.
3. Capture event constraints before back-and-forth begins.
4. Keep pricing private while still qualifying serious enquiries.

## Candidate product implications

- Personal domain may be essential for this vertical.
- Public pricing rules differ materially from Tattoo/Events.
- Availability is event-constraint based, not slot/calendar based.
- A media-kit-like one-pager is the core product.
