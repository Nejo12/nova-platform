# TAT-02 — Fine-line / botanical tattoo artist, Leipzig

**Status:** Interviewed  
**Evidence quality:** Strong  
**Source:** Founder-collected response

## Profile

Fine-line and botanical tattoo artist in Leipzig, ~6 years experience, ~14k followers, small shared studio. Mostly women 25–40. Roughly 3–4 tattoos/week.

## Discovery

- Instagram ~70%
- referrals from past clients ~20%
- Google Maps ~5%
- remainder walk-ins

## Current workflow

Instagram DM
→ ask for size / placement / references
→ quote range
→ client may disappear and return later
→ date agreed
→ PayPal deposit
→ Google Calendar

Recent booking required roughly nine messages.

## Required request information

- placement
- size in cm
- references
- colour / black
- cover-up status
- budget only when needed

## Repeated questions

- price
- pain
- books open?
- weekend availability?

## Current tools

Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, Google Calendar, PayPal, Notes for cancellation policy.

## Strong pain

### Inbox mixing
Friends, comments, price-checkers and serious leads share the same inbox.

### Lost serious enquiries
A real enquiry sat in Message Requests for 11 days and was lost to another artist.

### Existing form workaround fails
Linktree + Google Form exists, but clients ignore it and DM anyway.

### Books state does not stop enquiries
Even with BOOKS CLOSED in bio, 3–4 DMs/week still arrive.

### Flash double-booking
Flash is reserved through DMs and manually marked claimed. Artist reports double-booking a design twice.

### No-show cost
€50 PayPal deposit; no-shows about once/month still create lost capacity.

## Product reaction

Would switch if:
- it looked better than Linktree;
- it reduced low-quality “price?” DMs.

Would reject if:
- pages looked generic;
- own domain was impossible.

## Pricing evidence

- €8–10/month: trivial
- around €20/month: becomes a real decision

## Jobs-to-be-done

1. Separate serious tattoo requests from social inbox noise.
2. Capture required qualification information up front.
3. Keep books state clear.
4. Prevent flash double-booking.
5. Reduce lost enquiries and no-show cost.
