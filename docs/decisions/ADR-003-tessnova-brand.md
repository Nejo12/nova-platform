# ADR-003 — Adopt Tessnova as the M0 brand

**Status:** Accepted for M0

Use **Tessnova** as the approved working/commercial brand for product definition, Figma, validation materials and roadmap.

Use **`nova-platform`** as the engineering repository name.

T + E + S originated from Tattoo, Events and Speakers; Nova connects the product to the Nova family. The acronym is not a permanent product taxonomy.

Commercial launch remains gated on formal brand/domain/trademark review.
