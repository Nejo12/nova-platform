# ADR-003 — Adopt Tessnova as the product brand

**Status:** Accepted — Founder approved

## Decision

Use **Tessnova** as the approved commercial/product brand for product definition, Figma, validation materials, roadmap and implementation.

Use **`nova-platform`** as the engineering repository name.

## Origin

T + E + S originated from Tattoo, Events and Speakers; Nova connects the product to the Nova family.

The acronym is an origin story, not a permanent product taxonomy.

## Founder decision

The Founder reviewed the similarity between **Tessnova** and **Tesnova Solutions** and explicitly approved proceeding with Tessnova.

The existence of a similar commercial name is therefore **not a project blocker**.

## Legal note

Founder approval is a product/brand decision, not a legal opinion. If Tessnova is later filed as a registered trademark, perform the appropriate professional trademark due diligence at that stage.
